How Virus Spread

How Contagion Coronavirus

The coronavirus primarily spreads through respiratory droplets released when an infected person coughs, sneezes, talks, or breathes. It can also spread via contact with contaminated surfaces, making close contact with infected individuals a significant risk factor.

  • Human Contact

    COVID-19 spreads primarily through close human contact via respiratory droplets and surfaces.

  • Air Transmission

    COVID-19 spreads through inhaling airborne droplets and aerosols released by infected individuals nearby.

  • Contaminated Objects

    COVID-19 can be transmitted through contact with contaminated objects, leading to potential infection risks.

Corona Virus Symptoms

Common COVID-19 symptoms include fever, cough, shortness of breath, fatigue, and loss of taste. These symptoms can vary in severity and may appear 2-14 days after exposure to the virus

Prevention Coronavirus

Preventing coronavirus involves vaccination, wearing masks, practicing hand hygiene, maintaining physical distance, and improving indoor ventilation.

  • Wash Your Hands For 20sec

    Practice regular hand hygiene by washing hands frequently with soap and water or using hand sanitizer.

  • Cover Nose and Mouth When Sneezing

    Maintain physical distancing of at least six feet from others to minimize exposure.

  • Wear A Mask If Available

    Wear masks in crowded or enclosed spaces to reduce transmission risk.

Corona Virus Symptoms

COVID-19 shares symptoms with other respiratory illnesses like the flu and common cold, including cough, fever, and fatigue. However, unique symptoms such as loss of taste or smell can help distinguish it from these other conditions
